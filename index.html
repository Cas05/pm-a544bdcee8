How to play

Build a full XI in a 4-3-3 over eleven rounds. Each round you draw five cards: pick one and drop it on a glowing spot. Draft again as often as you like.

Positions. Players fit their main position and any alternate positions EA lists (FUT style). Tap a placed card, then another spot, to move or swap it.

Talent comes from the card's rarity, set by its FC 27 overall rating (OVR). Each rarity has its own shape:

Bronze · ≤69 OVR · 3
Silver · 70–75 · 5
Gold · 76–80 · 8
Platinum · 81–85 · 11
Hero · 86+ · 15

Chemistry comes from the 18 links between neighbouring spots, plus a bonus for each well-linked player. Score = Talent + Chem points. The Chemistry x/33 in the header is FUT-style: each player shows 0–3 diamonds for the share of their possible link points they have earned.

  • Same club: green link, +2
  • Same league and same country: green link, +2
  • Same league or same country: yellow link, +1
  • No match: red link, 0 (each link maxes out at +2)
  • Player with 2+ link points: +6
  • Player with 4+ link points: +11 (a green link counts as 2)
